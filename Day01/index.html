About Bodh Gaya

Bodh Gaya is one of the most important Buddhist pilgrimage destinations in the world.

It is famous for the Mahabodhi Temple and its peaceful surroundings.

Places to Visit

Mahabodhi Temple

The Mahabodhi Temple is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a major pilgrimage destination for Buddhists from around the world.

Great Buddha Statue

The Great Buddha Statue is a magnificent sculpture that represents the serene and peaceful nature of the place.

Thai Temple

The Thai Temple is a beautiful example of Thai architecture and a peaceful place for meditation and reflection.
